**Last updated 23 September 2026**

This notice explains how Ten X Africa (Pty) Ltd handles personal information. We build and run software for owner-led businesses, so almost everything we hold is business contact information: names, work email addresses and what people tell us about how their business runs.

## Who we are

Ten X Africa (Pty) Ltd is the responsible party (the "controller" under GDPR) for the information described here.

- Registration number 2020/714539/07
- VAT number 4250317601
- Registered in South Africa; we work from Johannesburg

Our information officer, and the address to write to about anything in this notice, is [hello@tenxafrica.co.za](mailto:hello@tenxafrica.co.za).

## What we collect

**When you use the contact form:** your name, work email, company, country, what takes the most time each week, your message, and whether you ticked the consent box.

**When you take the Digital Maturity Score:** your answers to the twelve questions, the score they produce, and the contact details you enter with them (name, work email, company, website, company size, sector, country and your biggest weekly time-sink), plus whether you agreed to be contacted.

**When you book the assessment call:** the details the booking form asks for (name, email, company name, website, company size and country, and your biggest weekly time-sink), the time you chose, and the notes we make on the call.

**When we email each other:** your business email address, what you write, and any details in your email signature. If we found your business through our own research, we hold the business contact details we found in public sources such as your company website. We never buy email lists.

**When you browse this site:** Google Analytics records pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type, and how you arrived, using cookies. Cloudflare, which protects and delivers the site, processes your IP address and technical request data to block abuse. The security check on our forms (Cloudflare Turnstile) looks at browser signals to tell people from bots. We remember whether you dismissed the cookie notice in your browser's local storage.

## Why we use it, and on what legal basis

- **To reply to you and to prepare for a call you booked.** Basis: taking steps at your request before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.
- **To score your assessment and follow up on it.** Basis: your request, and your consent where you ticked the box.
- **To contact businesses that look like a good fit, by email, once.** Basis: our legitimate interest in business-to-business contact under UK and EU GDPR, and the equivalent rules in the US, Australia, New Zealand and the UAE. For South African recipients we follow POPIA section 69: one approach asking for consent, and further contact only if you give it.
- **To deliver and support work you have hired us for.** Basis: our contract with you or your business.
- **To keep records the law requires,** such as invoices and tax records. Basis: legal obligation.
- **To understand how the site is used and keep it secure.** Basis: our legitimate interest in running a working, safe website, and your consent where your local law requires it for analytics cookies.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. The assessment score is arithmetic on your own answers; it decides nothing about you.

## Who handles it for us

We use a small number of service providers, each bound by their own data processing terms:

- **Microsoft 365** — email, calendar, Microsoft Bookings, Teams calls and document storage.
- **Our customer records system** — the database where we keep contacts, companies, notes and tasks. We run it ourselves on a server we rent from **DigitalOcean**.
- **Cloudflare** — website protection and delivery, the Turnstile security check, and the small service that receives form and assessment submissions and writes them to our records.
- **GitHub Pages** — hosts this website's pages.
- **Google** — Google Analytics for site statistics, and Google Fonts for the typefaces on this site.

We also use software tools, including AI assistants, to research businesses, draft emails and keep our records tidy. They work on the same information described here, and we are responsible for everything sent in our name.

## International transfers

We are based in South Africa, and our providers store and process information in other countries, including the United States and the European Union. Where information leaves South Africa, the UK or the EU, we rely on the provider's contractual safeguards (such as the standard contractual clauses) and on POPIA section 72.

## How long we keep it

- Enquiries, assessment results and call notes where no work follows: up to 24 months from our last contact, then deleted.
- Client project records and correspondence: for the length of the engagement plus 5 years.
- Invoices and accounting records: as long as South African tax law requires, currently 5 years.
- Opt-outs: we keep your email address and company on a do-not-contact list for as long as we operate, because that is the only way to make sure we never contact you again.
- Analytics data: Google Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months.

## Your rights

Depending on where you are, you can ask us to:

- tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy;
- correct it or delete it;
- stop or restrict using it, including objecting to direct marketing at any time;
- give it to you in a portable format;
- withdraw consent you gave, without affecting what we did before.

Email [hello@tenxafrica.co.za](mailto:hello@tenxafrica.co.za). We reply within one month, and we may ask you to confirm who you are first. There is no charge.

## How to complain

Please tell us first, and we will try to put it right. You can also complain to a regulator:

- **South Africa:** the Information Regulator, [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za).
- **United Kingdom:** the Information Commissioner's Office, [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).
- **Elsewhere:** the data protection authority where you live or work.

## Security

Access to our records is limited to the Ten X Africa team and the systems that run our routines. We use encrypted connections, strong sign-in protection on our accounts, and keep our own server updated. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

## Changes to this notice

If we change how we use personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
